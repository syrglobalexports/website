## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an institutional, high-trust maritime export ethos. It bridges the authority of international cross-border logistics with modern commitments to sustainable, biodegradable, and agricultural trade. 

The aesthetic is grounded in corporate architectural precision: high structural fidelity, clean grid alignments, crisp separation of data, and deliberate contrast. It evokes the permanence of maritime freight, the precision of legal cross-border commerce, and the responsibility of eco-conscious global supply chains. Visual motifs draw from container geometry, nautical longitude grids, and certified compliance stamps.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative maritime presence, supported by premium trade accents and environmental verification hues:

- **Primary (`#0F2854`)**: Deep Trade Navy. Serves as the bedrock color for primary navigation, hero typography, dominant interactive elements, and key structural headers.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27`)**: Sovereign Ochre / Gold. Extracted from trade certification lines and nautical instruments, used sparingly for accents, key call-to-action highlights, premium tier indicators, and active focal markers.
- **Tertiary (`#1B4D3E`)**: Forest Botanical Green. Reserved for sustainability badges, biodegradable product tags, carbon-neutral shipping badges, and environmental certifications.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`)**: Deep Slate. Used for high-contrast, fatigue-free body text and structural borders against crisp white canvas backdrops (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an uncompromising standard of legitimacy. Manrope provides geometric precision and architectural heft to headlines, anchoring trade stats and primary section titles. Inter provides absolute legibility for manifests, technical specifications, tariffs, and global logistics tables. 

All eyebrow tags, status markers, and product category indicators utilize uppercase tracking with `label-md` or `label-sm` to reinforce an official maritime documentation aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column corporate grid for desktop (maximum width 1320px) and collapses to a 6-column grid on tablet (768px - 1024px) and 4 columns on mobile (&lt; 768px). 

Rhythms reflect disciplined containerization: spacious module margins prevent dense shipping data from feeling cluttered. Section paddings step uniformly in factors of 8px, prioritizing vertical legibility and sharp alignment along column edges. Data tables and catalog matrices lock into rigid horizontal partitions to facilitate multi-metric scanning across international procurement teams.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with understated physical restraint rather than diffuse digital abstraction:

- **Surface Layers**: Primary background sits at pure `#FFFFFF` with alternating technical sections resting on `#F8FAFC`. Contrast separators utilize hairline borders (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Shadows**: Shadows are cast with a cool navy tint to maintain chromatic unity with `#0F2854`. A standard card rests on `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.04)`.
- **Hover & Interactive Elevation**: On card hover or flyout trigger, the shadow elevates to `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.05)`, accompanied by a 1px border shift to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Modals & Drawers**: Deep overlay backdrop (`rgba(11, 27, 61, 0.6)`) with high-depth projection: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 40, 84, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, conservative curvature (`roundedness: 1`). Elements have a baseline border radius of `0.25rem` (4px), extending to `0.5rem` (8px) for cards and modals. 

This restrained geometry preserves the industrial, containerized nature of global freight and commercial shipping documents, avoiding playful or consumer-oriented roundness while softening harsh utilitarian corners just enough for modern digital interfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#0F2854` background, `#FFFFFF` text, subtle 4px radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover state deepens to `#0A1C3B` with gold focus outline (`2px solid #C59B27`).
- **Accent / RFQ Action**: Solid `#C59B27` background with `#FFFFFF` or `#0F2854` bold text, designed specifically for trade inquiries and "Request a Quote".
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1px solid `#0F2854` border, transparent background, `#0F2854` text. Fills with `#F1F5F9` on hover.
- **Eco / Sustainability Action**: `#1B4D3E` solid background with pure white text, indicating certified organic or green export lines.

### Chips & Badges
- **Specification Chips**: Pill or 4px-radius badges. Light slate background (`#F1F5F9`) with `#1E293B` text and `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Sustainable Badge**: `#E8F5E9` background, `#1B4D3E` text, decorated with an organic verification indicator.
- **Grade & Quality Badge**: Subtle `#FEF9C3` background, `#854D0E` gold text for export grading (e.g., "Grade A Premium", "ISO 9001 Certified").

### Cards (Product & Service)
- Bounded by 1px solid `#E2E8F0` on white, with 8px (`rounded-lg`) curvature. Includes an upper metadata band displaying HS Codes, packaging standards, or country of origin, set apart by an interior divider rule. Top or corner accent border of 2px `#C59B27` applied to featured export contracts.

### Form Inputs & Inquiry Fields
- Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `0.25rem` corner radius, and 12px 16px internal padding. Labels are rendered in `label-md` (`#1E293B`). Focus states utilize a crisp, high-visibility navy ring (`#0F2854`) paired with an ochre glow.

### Data Tables & Manifests
- Crisp borders separating rows, alternating line background `#F8FAFC`, header row filled with `#0F2854` featuring white uppercase `label-md` typography. Cells strictly aligned right for numerical weights/volumes and left for descriptions.
